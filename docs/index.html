What is real: the Sudair fence (OpenStreetMap, measured 32.3 km), the terrain (Copernicus DEM GLO-30), the imagery (Esri) and the zoning come from the project repository. FEBUS A1-NEO interrogator, 2 active channels from the control building.

What is simulated: every event. The AI step uses the capstone's AI interface as a stub: correct class with probability Pc = 0.983, the low end of 0.983–0.993 (Wang et al., 2024, via Shao et al., 2025). The real ViT-1D classifier was trained and tested on Brno data only. Localisation 4–6 m and the camera link rates are simulation results.

Imagery: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics. Terrain: Copernicus DEM GLO-30. Data: OpenStreetMap contributors. Vertical exaggeration is for display only.
